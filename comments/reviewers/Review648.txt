Reviewer 1 of ROMAN 2026 submission 362

Comments to the author
======================

The paper presents a clear and practical method for
incorporating motion cues into skeleton-based action
anticipation. The idea of sampling optical flow around
keypoints is intuitive, and the improvements in early
prediction performance are consistent and meaningful. The
paper is also well structured and easy to follow.

That said, the contribution appears incremental, and it
would benefit from clearer positioning of novelty relative
to existing pose-motion fusion methods. Additionally, the
evaluation could be strengthened by including stronger
baselines and deeper analysis of failure cases and
robustness (e.g., to pose noise or global motion
scenarios).
